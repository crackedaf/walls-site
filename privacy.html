WALLS / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026.

Walls is an Android app for wallpapers and home-screen widgets, made by crackedaf ("we"). This page says what Walls collects, why, who handles it, and how to delete it.

In short. You can use Walls without an account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data. Most of what the widgets use stays on your phone. What does leave it is listed below, and you can delete all of it at once.

What leaves your phone

  • Your account, if you make oneYour email address and an account ID. With Google sign-in, also the name and profile picture link Google shares. A password is stored only as a hash, so we can't read it. Sign-in records your IP address in our backend's security logs. Used to sign you in and to keep your saved wallpapers.
  • Saved wallpapers, when signed inWhich wallpapers you saved, so they follow you to another phone.
  • Approximate location, only for My locationIf you set a Weather widget to My location, Walls reads your phone's approximate location, never the precise one. The coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the forecast (and the air quality, for an Air quality widget), and your phone's geocoding service (Google's on most Android phones) turns them into a town name. Walls keeps only the latest area on the phone, not a history. It checks in the background only if you choose "Allow all the time".
  • Weather for a cityThe coordinates of the city you picked go to Open-Meteo, for the forecast and, with an Air quality widget, the air quality.
  • AI & Agents widgets, only if you pair a PCThe Walls script on your PC sends, linked to your account: the PC's name, the state of each Claude Code session, short step labels (such as "Editing HomeWidgets.kt", "Running git push", short search terms or a website's name), the project folder's name, token counts, plan usage and working time. It never sends your prompts, code, file contents, full paths or command arguments. Only the latest report is kept. Removing the PC in Walls deletes it and its reports.
  • DownloadsWallpapers are downloaded from our backend. Like any server, it sees your IP address while it answers.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos you pick for generators or the Photo widget (Walls keeps a small copy for the widget), and contacts you pick for the Contact widget. They come from Android's own pickers, so Walls never reads your whole gallery or contact list.
  • Your step count, battery readings, water and habit logs, the Wi-Fi name and password you enter for Share Wi-Fi, and the UPI ID and name you enter for Pay me QR.
  • The state the control widgets show (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, torch and the rest), your widget settings, the wallpapers you set and your appearance choices.
  • For frosted glass widgets: a small copy of your home-screen wallpaper (one Walls set, a picture you choose, or, on Android 12 and older if you allow it, the wallpaper itself), blurred on the phone behind each widget, and where each widget sits on the screen. Android's storage permission is used only to read the wallpaper.

Walls turns off Android's app backup, so none of this is copied to your Google backup. Uninstalling Walls removes it.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no selling or sharing of data for advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites. Walls never asks for precise location, the camera, the microphone, phone access, your contacts list, SMS, call logs, notification access or accessibility. If you installed Walls from Google Play and chose to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may send us anonymous crash reports; that sharing is controlled by Google, not by Walls.

Who handles it

  • SupabaseHosts our backend: accounts, saved wallpapers, agent widget data and the wallpaper catalogue. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Open-MeteoWeather forecasts and air quality. Receives the coordinates and your IP address with each request. Open-Meteo privacy policy.
  • GoogleGoogle sign-in, if you use it, and geocoding on most Android phones. Google privacy policy.
  • An email serviceSends sign-in, confirmation and password reset emails to your address.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Fonts and jsDelivrHost and serve this page and the account deletion page, and see your IP address while doing it.

They process data only to provide these services. We don't give your data to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Delete it in the app (You, then Delete account) or, without the app, on the account deletion page. Your account, saved wallpapers, paired PCs and their reports are deleted at once. PC pairing codes expire after 10 minutes. Our providers' logs and backups may keep copies for a limited time under their own policies, then delete them.

Security

Everything Walls sends uses HTTPS. Your sign-in session is encrypted on the phone with a key kept in Android's Keystore. Database rules let each account read only its own data, and pairing codes and PC tokens are stored only as hashes.

Children

Walls is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can use Walls without an account, refuse or revoke any permission in Android's settings, and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to get a copy of your data or to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you more rights; email us to use them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. For a significant change, we will also say so in the app or on its Google Play listing.
